Yes. We can move you to a larger plan with minimal downtime. Contact support before your next billing date and we will help plan the change.

Do you manage the server for me?

These are self-managed VPS plans with monitoring and support. We can help with common setup questions; ongoing application administration is scoped separately.

What does annual billing mean?

Annual billing gives you 12 months of service for the price of 10 months. The cards show the effective monthly rate and the full annual total.

Which payment methods can I use?

We accept bank transfer, debit or credit card, and local mobile wallets. The checkout team will confirm the best option for your account.
