# PKHosting pricing audit

Observed on the public PKHosting homepage on 14 November 2026. These observations describe the live page, not this invented VPS offer.

1. **Top promotional strip:** the “BLACK FRIDAY SALE” banner puts first-year offers before the core navigation. **Consequence:** a visitor gets an immediate price anchor, but the promotional message competes with the task of finding the right product.
2. **Currency switcher in the hosting section:** the page says prices update automatically and invites a currency switch. **Consequence:** local and international shoppers can compare without leaving the page, but dynamic prices need a clear loading/failure state.
3. **Hosting plan cards:** Personal, Business and Professional cards show first-year price, renewal price and “Order Now”. **Consequence:** showing renewal beside the discount reduces surprise at checkout, although the card hierarchy is denser than a single headline price.
4. **“Most Popular” label:** Business Hosting is explicitly marked as the popular choice. **Consequence:** it provides a useful decision shortcut, but it can bias scanning toward popularity instead of workload fit.
5. **Feature lists on cards:** cards combine limits (websites, storage, email) with service features such as migration and support. **Consequence:** benefits are easy to scan, while mixed units make a side-by-side technical comparison harder.
6. **Daily backups link:** the backup bullet links to a separate policy and the page distinguishes retention periods between plans. **Consequence:** the link supports informed buying, but an important term requires leaving the pricing comparison.

This assessment page borrows the useful patterns (visible billing context, popular-plan cue and explicit inclusions) while adding a dedicated comparison table for VPS specifications.
